# The weekly review

Once a week, go through every open item:

1. Empty the inbox into real days or drop items.
2. Look at every task that moved more than three times: split it, park it, or delete it.
3. Check the calendar of the coming week and place the big blocks first.

A weekly review keeps the list honest. Items that survive several reviews
without moving are not tasks, they are decisions waiting to be made.

Small chores found during the review fall under the
[[knowledge/two-minute-rule|two-minute rule]].
