# The two-minute rule

If a chore takes less than two minutes, do it immediately. Writing it down,
sorting it and reading it again tomorrow costs more than the chore itself.

The rule fights procrastination on small things: a list made of thirty tiny
chores looks heavy, and a heavy list is the first thing we avoid opening.

- Reply to a short message: two minutes, do it.
- Book the dentist: two minutes, do it.
- Plan the kitchen budget: not two minutes, it goes to the list.

The rule pairs well with a [[knowledge/weekly-review|weekly review]]: the review
catches what the two-minute rule could not finish.
